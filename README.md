# 🤖 AllenAI

### Your AI Study & Business Assistant

**AllenAI** is an AI-powered assistant designed to help students understand school subjects, improve their learning, and explore useful ideas for business and everyday tasks.

## 📚 School Work & Learning

AllenAI can help students with subjects such as:

* Mathematics
* Science
* Geography
* Spanish
* English
* Social Studies
* Religious Education
* Computer Studies

AllenAI is designed to explain topics in simple language, show step-by-step working when appropriate, provide examples, and help students understand **why** an answer is correct.

### Example Questions

**Mathematics**

> Solve 3x + 5 = 20 and show the working.

**Science**

> Explain photosynthesis in simple words.

**Geography**

> What is plate tectonics?

**Spanish**

> Explain how Spanish -ar verbs work.

**Computer Studies**

> What is the difference between hardware and software?

## 🎯 Goal

The goal of AllenAI is to create a useful assistant that can support **learning, creativity, problem-solving, and productivity**.

AllenAI is meant to help people learn and work more effectively while still encouraging them to understand the information for themselves.
